### Want to participate in an experiment?

We are recruiting participants for our studies!

1) **Description**: Participation in this study involves making reaching movements while playing simple computer games. Your participation will help us advance our understanding of human motor control and motor learning.

2) **Eligibility**: We are looking for healthy volunteers aged 18-35 with normal or corrected-to-normal vision (i.e., 20/20 with glasses or contacts) and no known history of neurological impairment.

3) **Duration**: The study will consist of 1-2 sessions and will take approximately 1.5 to 2 hours per session.  

4) **Compensation**: Participants will receive a $15 honorarium per session.

5) **Location:** Room 203, Osborne Ctr Unit 2, 6108 Thunderbird Blvd, UBC Vancouver Campus

**If you are interested, please contact us at ccm.lab@ubc.ca.** We will be in touch with more information and scheduling.
