### Join the lab

We are always looking for highly motivated and curious graduate students and postdoctoral fellows to join us. If you have a strong interest in sensorimotor control and learning, this is a great environment to conduct research in those areas. 

If interested, please send an email to <a href="mailto:hyosub.kim@ubc.ca">Hyosub</a> that includes the following:

1) Statement of interest - include why you are interested in joining this lab, prior research experience, and relevant coursework   
2) CV    
3) Transcripts (does not need to be an official copy)   
4) 2-3 references
