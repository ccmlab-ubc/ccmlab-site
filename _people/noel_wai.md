### About me ###

I am a 5th-year undergraduate Neuroscience student in the Cognitive & Behavioural stream, and I’m fascinated by how the brain processes sensory inputs and unconsciously prioritises them. My background involves working in Fraser Health hospitals and volunteering in care centres, where I observed many Neuro Rehab and MSK patients rebuilding balance and motor function. Currently, I’m a Directed Studies (NSCI 448) student in the CCM Lab. Through this work, I hope to understand more about how passive and active proprioceptive information can guide behaviour, particularly in sensorimotor learning. Outside the lab, I enjoy solo hiking, watching sports, and baking with friends.
