### About me ###
I am an undergraduate Neuroscience student in the Cellular & Molecular stream at UBC. Previously, I completed an NSERC USRA term and directed studies (NSCI 448) in the Moritz Lab, where my project focused on the effects of metabolic disruption on rod photoreceptor disc synthesis. Through this work, I developed a strong interest in how the brain processes sensory information. More recently, I’ve become interested in how sensory information is used to guide behavior, particularly in motor control and sensorimotor learning, and I’m excited to apply computational approaches to model these processes in the CCM Lab. In my free time, I enjoy baking, running, and exploring new cafes around Vancouver.
