### About me ###
I am majoring in Kinesiology and minoring in Psychology. I’ve been fascinated by how simple behaviour, like reaching movements, arises from complex processes in the brain. My passion and curiosity in cognition and motor control have led me to the alley of research, and taking Dr.Kim’s Bayesian modelling course has given me further insights into the human mind and to view human behaviour through a computational lens. I am excited to work in the lab and to use computational modelling to understand the relationship between perception and action. Outside of academia, I enjoy drawing and trying new recipes.
